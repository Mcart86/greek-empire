Campus Ambassador Program

Fraternity. Sorority. Built for the brothers and sisters who live it.

Greek Empire is a custom apparel company built specifically for sororities and fraternities.

As a Campus Ambassador, you'll help connect chapters at your school with Greek Empire for custom apparel and merchandise, while gaining experience in sales, marketing, networking, and entrepreneurship.

You'll build relationships on campus, help organizations create custom merchandise, and earn commission on orders you bring in.

3 Ways to Get Paid

01

Commission on orders

Earn 10% on every order placed using your referral code. No cap. Every chapter you refer also earns 2.5%–5% back on qualifying orders.

02

Social Media Incentive (SIP)

Earn bonuses by creating approved content on TikTok and Instagram. Submit through the Greek Empire content portal before posting.

03

Ambassador referrals

Earn 2.5% commission when qualified students you refer join the Campus Ambassador Sign Up Form.
